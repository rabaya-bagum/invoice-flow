## What we collect
- **Account:** your email address, name and a password (the password is handled by our authentication
  provider and is never visible to us in readable form).
- **Business information you enter:** business name, address, tax number, logo, signature, payment
  instructions and invoice settings.
- **Your customers' information that you enter:** names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, notes. You are
  responsible for having the right to store it. For this data we act as your processor.
- **Invoices and payments:** line items, amounts, dates, statuses, and payment records (amount, method,
  status, Stripe references). We do **not** receive or store card numbers.
- **Device and technical data:** push notification token, IP address and request metadata (for security,
  abuse prevention and rate limiting), and an audit log of sensitive account actions.

## How we use it
To provide the app (create, send and track invoices, take payments), send notifications you can turn
